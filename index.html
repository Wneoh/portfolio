Featured project / AI support workspace

RelayDesk

Your AI team. Your final say.

A support platform for small businesses with one or two administrators and multiple AI specialists. Customers submit tickets; Support, Orders, and Billing agents investigate using approved knowledge and permitted actions, then propose a resolution for a person to review.

  • Human oversight. Support reviewers approve with private notes. Manual reruns create editable drafts, and every ticket closure needs human approval.
  • Reliable operations. Live conversations, durable AI jobs, response deadlines, and overdue alerts keep work moving.
  • Measurable performance. Chart.js reports compare specialist workloads, review outcomes, and response and resolution times, with CSV export.

Server-side permissions and concurrency checks protect assignments and approvals when two people act on the same ticket.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Node.js / Express
  • PostgreSQL
  • Socket.IO
  • Chart.js

Demo screenshots use scripted responses. Live AI is an optional, separately configured mode.

Full-stack e-commerce / Store operations

Bancom

From product discovery to store operations.

A responsive electronics store covering both sides of commerce: a clear shopping experience for customers and practical tools for the people running the business. Built as a server-rendered Express application with EJS and MongoDB.

  • Customer journey. Search and filter 24 fictional products, browse image galleries, manage a persistent bag, and use bag or quick checkout with simulated payments.
  • Store management. Edit products and images, inspect customer orders, customise the homepage, and choose from five shared themes.
  • Sales insight. Chart.js reports show order trends, average order value, best sellers, and category performance with date filters.

Checkout recalculates prices on the server, stores order snapshots, and uses unique checkout keys to handle repeat submissions. Currency conversion is for display; saved orders remain in MYR.

  • Node.js
  • Express
  • MongoDB
  • EJS
  • Chart.js

Fictional demo catalogue · Simulated payments; no real money is charged.

Native Android / Community journal

LitS

Stories, photos and conversations in one personal space.

A refreshed Java Android app for readers and writers to share moments, poems and ideas. Modernised for Android 16 with a consistent interface, four appearance themes and a richer post experience.

  • Photo storytelling. Up to six photos per entry, compact feed mosaics, swipe galleries and full-screen viewing.
  • Community interaction. Five emoji reactions and comments with names, timestamps and a persistent reply composer.
  • Thoughtful writing. Word counts, image previews, individual photo removal and cancellable uploads.
  • Personal appearance. Classic, Forest, Sepia and Midnight themes saved on the device.
  • Java
  • AndroidX
  • Material Components
  • Firebase
  • Android 16

Screenshots show the local design preview. Live publishing, reactions and comments require Firebase database reactivation and access-rule configuration.
